How it works

The calculator adds your per-load laundry costs (washing, drying, detergent, water, and extras), then multiplies that single load rate by your weekly load count:

Cost Per Load = Washer Cost + Dryer Cost + Supplies Cost + Water Cost + Other Cost

Weekly Cost = Cost Per Load × Loads Per Week
Yearly Cost = Weekly Cost × 52 Weeks
Monthly Cost = Yearly Cost ÷ 12 Months (or Weekly Cost × 4.345 average weeks per month)

What the result means

Cost per load shows the estimated cost for one laundry load. Weekly, monthly, and yearly totals show how that per-load cost adds up over time. Largest cost part shows which single cost input contributes the most.

Limitations

This is a simple manual-cost estimate. It does not calculate electricity usage directly from wattage, machine efficiency ratings, local utility tariffs, or product container sizes automatically. It does not convert currencies.

This tool is for personal estimation only and does not provide certified financial, tax, legal, or appliance efficiency advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my detergent cost per load?

Divide the package price by the total number of loads listed on the container. For example, a detergent bottle priced at $15.00 that yields 60 loads costs $0.25 per load ($15.00 ÷ 60).

What should I enter for washer and dryer costs if I wash at home?

For home machines, washer and dryer costs represent the electricity, gas, or water consumed per cycle. You can estimate machine electricity using our Electricity Cost Calculator. If you use a laundromat or shared building laundry room, enter the exact coin or card vend price per cycle.

What if I air-dry my laundry and do not use a machine dryer?

Leave the Dryer cost per load field set to 0. The calculator will accurately compute your total based only on washing machine use, detergent, and water costs.

Does the currency selector convert between currencies?

No. The currency selector only updates the display symbol (such as $, £, or €). Calculations are purely numerical based on the exact amounts you type in.
