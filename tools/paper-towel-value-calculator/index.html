How to calculate paper towel unit price

Paper towel brands use roll nicknames like "Double Plus," "Triple Roll," and "6 = 15 Regular Rolls" that vary from one manufacturer to another. To find the real unit price on any grocery or warehouse club shelf, check the fine print near the bottom front corner of the package for the actual roll count, sheets per roll, and total square feet.

Cost per roll = Final Package Price ÷ Rolls in Package

Total sheets = Rolls in Package × Sheets per Roll

Cost per 100 sheets = (Final Package Price ÷ Total Sheets) × 100

Price per square foot = Final Package Price ÷ Total Square Feet (multiply by 100 for shelf-tag cost per 100 sq ft)

Worked example: 8 paper towel rolls for $18.16

Suppose a pack of 8 paper towel rolls costs $18.16, with 110 sheets per roll and 450.5 total square feet printed on the front label:

  • Unit price per roll: $18.16 ÷ 8 = $2.27 per roll.
  • Total sheets: 8 × 110 = 880 total sheets.
  • Cost per 100 sheets: ($18.16 ÷ 880) × 100 = $2.06 per 100 sheets.
  • Price per square foot: $18.16 ÷ 450.5 = $0.040/sq ft ($4.03 per 100 sq ft).

Select-a-Size vs. Full-Sheet: why square footage matters

Comparing paper towels by cost per 100 sheets alone can be misleading if one pack uses Select-a-Size (half-sheet) perforations and the other uses Full-Sheet cuts. A Select-a-Size roll often lists 110 to 160 smaller sheets, while a Full-Sheet roll with the exact same amount of paper may list only 55 to 80 sheets.

Entering the total square feet printed on both packages removes that distortion completely. Price per 100 square feet also matches the unit-price format shown on many US grocery shelf tags, making bulk comparisons fast and accurate.

Limitations

This calculator measures unit price based only on the numbers you enter. It does not measure paper absorbency, wet strength, ply thickness (1-ply vs. 2-ply), or how many sheets your household uses per spill—a stronger towel can sometimes save money if you use fewer sheets. US benchmark ranges are general estimates for informational purposes only, and the tool does not automatically add sales tax, shipping, or live currency conversion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Paper Towel Value FAQs

Practical answers on unit price per roll, Select-a-Size sheets, square-foot math, and roll labels.

A pack of 8 paper towel rolls costs $18.16. What is the unit price?

Divide the total pack price ($18.16) by the number of rolls (8). The unit price is $2.27 per roll ($18.16 / 8 = $2.27).

Why is price per square foot more accurate than cost per sheet for paper towels?

Select-a-Size or Pick-a-Size rolls use half-size perforations, so they show nearly twice as many sheets as Full-Sheet rolls for the same amount of paper. Comparing price per square foot (or per 100 sq ft) measures total paper area fairly.

What is a good price per square foot for paper towels in the US?

For 2-ply paper towels in the US, a strong stock-up deal is generally under $0.022 per square foot (under $2.20 per 100 sq ft). Typical everyday retail pricing usually runs between $0.023 and $0.034 per square foot ($2.30 to $3.40 per 100 sq ft).

How do you calculate cost per 100 paper towel sheets?

Multiply the number of rolls by sheets per roll to get total sheets in the pack. Then divide total pack price by total sheets and multiply by 100: Cost per 100 sheets = (Total Price / Total Sheets) x 100.

What do "Double Plus" and "Triple Roll" labels actually mean?

Roll nicknames like Double, Double Plus, Triple, and Mega are not standardized across brands. Each brand compares its roll to its own small regular-roll baseline. Checking total square feet and total sheets printed on the front label shows the real quantity.
