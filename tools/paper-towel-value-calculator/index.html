How it works

Paper towel packaging terms like "Double Plus Roll" or "Mega Roll" are non-standardized brand marketing labels. This calculator normalizes any package into clear, comparable metrics:

Cost Per Roll = Package Price ÷ Rolls in Package
Total Sheets = Rolls in Package × Sheets Per Roll
Cost Per 100 Sheets = (Package Price ÷ Total Sheets) × 100
Cost Per Square Foot = Package Price ÷ Total Square Feet

Calculation example

Suppose a pack of 8 paper towel rolls costs $18.16, with 110 sheets per roll and 450.5 total square feet:

  • Unit Price Per Roll: $18.16 ÷ 8 = $2.27 per roll.
  • Total Sheets: 8 × 110 = 880 total sheets.
  • Cost Per 100 Sheets: ($18.16 ÷ 880) × 100 = $2.06 per 100 sheets.
  • Cost Per Square Foot: $18.16 ÷ 450.5 = $0.040 per square foot.

What the result means

Cost per roll gives you a general benchmark, but cost per 100 sheets and cost per square foot reveal the true value when comparing brands that cut roll thickness or sheet dimensions to simulate value.

Limitations

This calculator measures financial unit cost only. It does not measure sheet absorbency, ply thickness (1-ply vs 2-ply), paper tensile strength, or brand durability.

Frequently Asked Questions

A pack of 8 paper towel rolls costs $18.16. What is the unit price?

Divide the total pack price ($18.16) by the number of rolls (8). The unit price is $2.27 per roll ($18.16 ÷ 8 = $2.27).

How do you calculate cost per 100 paper towel sheets?

Multiply the number of rolls by the sheets per roll to find total sheets in the pack. Then divide the total package price by total sheets and multiply by 100: Cost per 100 sheets = (Total Price ÷ Total Sheets) × 100.

Why is cost per square foot more accurate for paper towels?

Brands frequently adjust sheet cut sizes between full-sheet cuts and "select-a-size" perforated cuts. Comparing price per total square foot normalizes these dimensions, ensuring an apples-to-apples comparison.

Does this tool save or upload my shopping data?

No. All calculations run strictly in your local browser session. No input data is stored in cookies, local storage, or sent to external servers.
