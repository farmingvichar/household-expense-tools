How to use

  1. Enter your monthly take-home income if you want to calculate remaining balance and expense percentage.
  2. Add the household expense categories that apply to your month.
  3. Leave unused expense fields blank. They will count as 0.
  4. Select calculate to view total expenses, remaining money, expense percentage, and your biggest spending category.

What this calculator includes

The form covers primary monthly household costs: housing (rent or mortgage), groceries, utilities, transportation, phone and internet, debt or loan payments, insurance or medical care, and general miscellaneous expenses.

Irregular or seasonal costs such as vehicle maintenance, annual subscriptions, childcare, or emergency repairs can be combined under Other expenses to keep monthly estimates balanced.

FAQ

Should I enter gross income or net take-home pay?

Enter your net take-home pay (after taxes and payroll deductions). Calculating expenses against the money actually deposited into your account gives an accurate picture of your true monthly budget.

How do I account for annual or irregular bills?

Divide annual or quarterly expenses (such as car registration or seasonal insurance premiums) by 12, then add that monthly portion into the Other expenses field.

Can I use this calculator without entering an income?

Yes. If you leave income blank, the tool still calculates your total monthly expenses. The expense percentage and remaining balance require an income figure to display.

Does this calculator save or store my financial data?

No. Calculations run entirely in your local browser session. No income figures or expense amounts are saved to localStorage or sent to any external server.

Are the calculations financial advice?

No. This tool provides basic mathematical summaries from the numbers you enter. It does not provide certified financial, accounting, tax, or investment advice.
