How it works

The calculator determines the percentage of purchased food that was discarded, then calculates the proportional monetary loss based on your original purchase price:

Wasted Percentage = (Wasted Quantity ÷ Total Quantity) × 100
Estimated Wasted Value = Total Cost × (Wasted Quantity ÷ Total Quantity)
Estimated Used Value = Total Cost − Estimated Wasted Value

Calculation example

Suppose you buy a 5 lb bag of apples for $10.00. If 1.5 lbs spoil and are discarded, the calculation is:

  • Wasted Percentage: (1.5 ÷ 5) × 100 = 30%
  • Wasted Value: $10.00 × 0.30 = $3.00 lost
  • Used Value: $10.00 − $3.00 = $7.00 consumed

What the result means

Estimated wasted value is the approximate dollar cost of the uneaten portion. Estimated used value is the portion of your purchase that was successfully consumed.

Limitations

This is a direct mathematical estimate. It assumes uniform cost distribution across the entire item weight or quantity. It does not convert between different unit dimensions (e.g., fluid volume to dry weight) or convert currency exchange rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate the cost of wasted food?

Divide the amount of food thrown away by the total amount purchased, then multiply that fraction by the original price paid. For example, if half of an $8.00 salad mix goes bad, your wasted food cost is $4.00 ($8.00 × 0.50).

What should I enter if an entire unopened item or package spoils?

Enter 1 for total quantity bought, 1 for wasted quantity, and choose "items" or "packs" as the unit. The wasted value will equal the full purchase price (100% waste).

Can I calculate wasted food cost for restaurant takeout or meal leftovers?

Yes. Enter the total meal price and use "items" or "packs" with an estimated fraction (for example, enter 1 for total quantity and 0.25 if approximately one-fourth of the dish was discarded).

Does this calculator convert units automatically?

No. You must enter both total quantity and wasted quantity in the same measurement unit (such as ounces with ounces, or pounds with pounds).
