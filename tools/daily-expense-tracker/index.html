    How Totals Are Calculated

    This tracker automatically tallies your entries across three distinct timeframes:

    Today Total

    Sums all transactions stamped with today's calendar date to monitor day-to-day spending limits.

    This Month Total

    Aggregates spending from the 1st of the current month to today, making it easy to pace monthly bills.

    All-Time History

    Keeps a running balance of your active log until you choose to remove individual rows or clear history.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Daily Expense Tracker FAQs

    Clear details on offline storage, privacy, and tracking household expenses.

    Is this expense tracker free and private to use?

    Yes. The tool is 100% free with no account setup or sign-in. Your entries are saved locally in your browser's private storage, meaning your personal financial numbers never leave your phone or computer.

    Will my expenses disappear if I close the browser tab?

    No. Entries are saved in your browser's localStorage. You can close the tab or turn off your device, and your numbers will still be here when you return.

    What happens if I clear my browser cookies or browsing history?

    Because localStorage is tied to your browser, clearing website storage or private browsing history will erase saved entries. For long-term records, jot down monthly totals before doing a full browser cleanup.

    Can I remove an accidental or incorrect entry?

    Yes. Each recorded item has its own delete button so you can remove mistakes instantly without resetting your entire log.

    Does this tracker convert different currencies?

    No currency conversions are performed. The tracker logs plain numbers so you can track in USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), INR (₹), or any currency you use at home.
