Helpful topics

What you can reach out about

Short, specific notes make it much easier to fix issues quickly.

  • Unexpected math results or rounding errors in any calculator
  • Broken links, slow pages, or mobile layout problems
  • Ideas for practical grocery, utility, or monthly budget tools
  • Questions about browser-only calculations and local storage
  • Corrections to unclear or outdated guide information

About replies

What to expect after you send a message

Household Expense Tools is maintained independently, so there is no live chat or phone desk. Every message is read, and replies usually take 2 to 3 business days when a follow-up is needed.

This website provides general household estimation tools only and cannot give personal financial, tax, legal, or accounting advice. For important money or legal decisions, please consult a qualified professional.
